NON-BINDING TERM SHEET — PHYSICAL POWER PURCHASE AGREEMENT

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — fictional parties and terms, used only to exercise the tooling in this repository.

Seller: Sunfield Solar II LLC. Buyer: Ridgeline Compute Partners LLC.
Project: 180 MWac solar photovoltaic facility with 90 MW / 360 MWh battery storage, Morgan County, Colorado.
Contract quantity: 100% of the project's energy, capacity and renewable energy credits.
Term: fifteen (15) years from the Commercial Operation Date.

<!-- page 2 -->
Price: $48.50 per MWh, fixed, with no escalation.
Guaranteed Commercial Operation Date: June 30, 2028. Seller pays delay damages of $150 per MW per day for up to 180 days after that date.
Performance guarantee: Seller guarantees annual production of at least 85% of expected energy; shortfall is paid at the difference between replacement cost and the contract price.
Credit support: Seller posts $18,000,000 before construction; Buyer posts a parent guaranty of $25,000,000.

<!-- page 3 -->
Curtailment: Buyer may curtail up to 5% of annual expected energy without compensation; beyond that Buyer pays the contract price for curtailed energy.
Exclusivity: Seller will negotiate exclusively with Buyer for sixty (60) days after signing this term sheet.
Binding provisions: only Confidentiality, Exclusivity and Governing Law (Colorado) are binding.
