GROUND LEASE AGREEMENT

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — all parties, parcels and terms are fictional and exist only to exercise the tooling in this repository.

This Ground Lease Agreement (the "Lease") is entered into as of March 3, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between Harlan Mesa Ranch LLC, a Colorado limited liability company ("Landlord"), and Ridgeline Compute Partners LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES. Landlord leases to Tenant approximately 212 acres of unimproved land in Weld County, Colorado, known as Parcel 7 and described in Exhibit A (the "Premises"), together with non-exclusive access easements over the ranch road shown on Exhibit B.

2. PERMITTED USE. Tenant may use the Premises for the development, construction, operation and maintenance of a data center campus, an on-site electrical substation, battery energy storage, and related facilities, and for no other purpose without Landlord's prior written consent.

<!-- page 2 -->
3. TERM.
3.1 Development Period. The Lease begins on the Effective Date with a development period of up to twenty-four (24) months (the "Development Period"), ending on the earlier of (a) the Commercial Operation Date or (b) the twenty-four month anniversary of the Effective Date.
3.2 Operating Term. The operating term is forty (40) years beginning on the day after the Development Period ends (the "Operating Term").
3.3 Extensions. Tenant may extend the Operating Term for two (2) successive periods of ten (10) years each by written notice delivered at least eighteen (18) months before expiration of the then-current term.

4. RENT.
4.1 Development Rent. During the Development Period Tenant shall pay annual rent of $600 per acre, payable in advance on each anniversary of the Effective Date.
4.2 Base Rent. Beginning on the first day of the Operating Term, Tenant shall pay annual Base Rent of $2,150 per acre, payable in equal quarterly installments.
4.3 Escalation. Base Rent increases by three percent (3.0%) on each anniversary of the start of the Operating Term.

<!-- page 3 -->
5. POWER AND INTERCONNECTION.
5.1 Tenant is solely responsible for obtaining interconnection service from Front Range Electric Cooperative or any successor utility, and for all costs of network upgrades assigned to the project.
5.2 Landlord shall grant, within thirty (30) days of Tenant's request, the transmission and distribution easements reasonably required for interconnection, in the form attached as Exhibit D.
5.3 If Tenant has not executed an interconnection agreement for at least 150 MW of load by the twenty-four (24) month anniversary of the Effective Date, Landlord may terminate this Lease on ninety (90) days' written notice, unless Tenant pays a one-time extension fee of $250,000, which extends that deadline by twelve (12) months.

6. WATER. Tenant shall use closed-loop or air-cooled systems. Tenant shall not draw more than 25 acre-feet of water per year from wells located on the Premises.

<!-- page 4 -->
7. ASSIGNMENT. Tenant may assign this Lease or sublet the Premises without Landlord's consent (a) to an affiliate or (b) to a lender or collateral agent as security. Any other assignment, including to a purchaser of the project, requires Landlord's consent in its sole discretion.

8. TERMINATION BY TENANT. Tenant may terminate this Lease at any time during the Development Period on sixty (60) days' written notice. Upon such termination Tenant shall pay a termination fee equal to one (1) year of Development Rent and shall restore the Premises as provided in Section 10.

9. DECOMMISSIONING SECURITY. Before commencing construction, Tenant shall deliver a letter of credit of $6,500,000 as security for its restoration obligations, adjusted every five (5) years to reflect an updated engineer's estimate.

10. RESTORATION. Within twenty-four (24) months after expiration or termination, Tenant shall remove above-ground improvements and restore the surface to substantially its pre-lease condition, excluding foundations more than three (3) feet below grade.

11. CONFIDENTIALITY. The terms of this Lease are confidential and may be disclosed only to a party's lenders, investors, advisors, and prospective purchasers who are bound by confidentiality obligations.

12. GOVERNING LAW. This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Colorado. Venue lies exclusively in the District Court for Weld County.

13. NOTICES. Notices must be in writing and delivered by nationally recognized overnight courier to the addresses in Exhibit C.
