FORM B — ONE-WAY NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (COMPANY AS DISCLOSER)

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — a fictional template used to exercise the tooling in this repository.

WHEN TO USE THIS FORM: Use Form B when only the company discloses confidential information, for example sharing site plans with a contractor, a prospective lender, a consultant, or an equipment vendor bidding on a project. If the counterparty will also share its own confidential information, use Form A instead.

1. The company ("Discloser") will provide Confidential Information to the counterparty ("Recipient") solely for the Purpose stated on the cover page.
2. Term. Confidentiality obligations last five (5) years from the date of last disclosure; trade secrets are protected for as long as they remain trade secrets.
3. Return or destruction within ten (10) business days of Discloser's written request.
4. No residuals clause. Recipient receives no license to Discloser's intellectual property.
5. Governing law: Colorado.
