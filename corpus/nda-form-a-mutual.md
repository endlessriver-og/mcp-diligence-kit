FORM A — MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — a fictional template used to exercise the tooling in this repository.

WHEN TO USE THIS FORM: Use Form A when BOTH parties will disclose confidential information to each other, for example joint development discussions, co-investment in a site, or a potential joint venture. Do not use Form A when only the company is disclosing (use Form B) or when the counterparty is a government body (use Form C, maintained by counsel).

1. Each party (as "Discloser") may share Confidential Information with the other (as "Recipient") solely to evaluate a potential transaction between them (the "Purpose").
2. Term. The agreement lasts two (2) years; confidentiality obligations survive for three (3) years after termination.
3. Non-solicitation. Neither party shall solicit the other's employees for twelve (12) months.

<!-- page 2 -->
4. Residuals. Neither party may use information retained in the unaided memory of its personnel.
5. Governing law: Colorado.
6. Signature blocks for both parties.
